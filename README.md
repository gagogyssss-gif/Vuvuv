# Nightshift: After Hours

Original mobile survival-horror prototype built with Godot 4.7.x.

Current vertical slice:
- 12 AM to 6 AM night loop (8 minutes)
- 8 security cameras
- left/right powered shutters
- service visor disguise mechanic
- limited flashlight battery
- office power management
- 8 original animatronics in Archive and Showline generations
- route-based AI with distinct counters
- procedural 3D office, corridors, stage and animatronic meshes
- touch UI plus keyboard controls
- Android export preset

Controls: A left shutter, D right shutter, M monitor, Space visor, F flashlight.

Archive characters: Bramble Stag, Rook Raccoon, Piper Owl, Mako Shark.
Showline characters: Nova Stag, Nova Raccoon, Nova Owl, Nova Shark.

The characters, models and names are intentionally original. The project uses the security-camera survival-horror genre without shipping copyrighted FNAF character models, textures, logos, audio, map art or names.

Production target: Godot 4.7.2 stable, GL Compatibility renderer for broad Android support.
